App Lock Revived is an open-source app locker for Android, rebuilt with Google's Material 3 Expressive design. It is a maintained fork of AppLock by Pranav Purwar.

<b>Locking</b>
* Lock any app with a PIN, pattern or password
* Fingerprint and face unlock, with an optional automatic prompt
* Accessibility, Usage stats or Shizuku detection, no root required
* Unlock duration, auto-lock for newly installed apps and a "Lock all now" Quick Settings tile
* Anti-uninstall protection

<b>Security</b>
* Brute-force lockout that doubles the cooldown after repeated wrong attempts
* Scrambled PIN keypad
* Screenshots blocked on lock screens
* PBKDF2-hashed credentials
* On-device unlock history

<b>Design</b>
* Material 3 Expressive throughout, with Google Sans Flex typography and spring motion
* Light, dark and system theme, dynamic color and pure black mode
* Themed adaptive icon

<b>Privacy</b>
Everything stays on your device. There are no ads, trackers or accounts. The only network request is an optional check for new releases on GitHub, and you can turn it off.
